### 5. Detailed Project Description (Executive Summary)
```
In corporate banking, reviewing a corporate credit facility dossier currently takes up to 11 days. Underwriters and Relationship Managers must manually cross-examine 70+ pages of audited financials, commercial registries, Shariah schedules, and legal filings. Conventional OCR and LLM tools process documents in isolation, completely missing cross-document contradictions and hidden liquidation liabilities.

SANAD (سند) is an institutional-grade forensic intelligence engine engineered specifically for Warba Bank’s Corporate Banking Group and Shariah Supervisory Board. 

Rather than outputting generic conversational answers, SANAD acts as an autonomous Senior Credit Forensic Analyst:
1. Multi-Document Circularization: Ingests audited financials, credit application affidavits, MOCI commercial registries, PACI geographic data, and Ministry of Justice gazettes, cross-examining them in under 38 seconds.
2. Forensic Conflict Detection (Ref #F-922): Identifies off-balance sheet liabilities and undisclosed sister-entity guarantees (e.g., catching a KWD 350,000 performance bond issued to an affiliate when the borrower declared zero contingent liabilities) and automatically drafts protective subordination clauses.
3. Automated AAOIFI Shariah & Taharah Engine: Screens P&L schedules for conventional interest income under AAOIFI Standard No. 21, calculates the exact Zakat and Taharah purification amounts, and pre-drafts remittance transfer orders for Bait Al-Zakat Kuwait.
4. Institutional Internal Memorandum Output: Formats the entire credit evaluation into Warba Bank’s official internal memorandum format (Copy No. 01, strictly confidential), complete with embedded liquidity intelligence metrics and verbatim verifiable citations.
5. Proactive RM Revenue Radar: Transforms compliance into balance-sheet growth by autonomously identifying maturity renewals, upsell opportunities (+KWD 700k expansion, earning KWD 39,375 p.a. bank margin), and trade LC cross-sells.
```

### 6. How It Works (Technical Architecture)
```
SANAD is built on a high-availability, decoupled financial architecture:

1. Ingestion & Sovereign Data Redaction:
   - Corporate PDFs, scans, and financial tables are ingested via the Document Workspace.
   - Client PII is sanitized according to Central Bank of Kuwait (CBK) data sovereignty directives prior to analytical processing.

2. Forensic Vector & Registry Circularization:
   - Chunked document vectors are circularized against mock live telemetry from the Ministry of Commerce & Industry (MOCI), Public Authority for Civil Information (PACI), and CiNet Credit Bureau.
   - The Discrepancy Engine runs cross-statement verification algorithms to compare claimed liabilities in credit applications against active court and gazette pledges.

3. Shariah Calculation & Purification Module:
   - Implements AAOIFI Shariah Standard No. 21 rules engine to screen conventional income thresholds (<5% ceiling, <30% debt-to-assets ceiling).
   - Generates automated Bait Al-Zakat purification schedules with solar/lunar calendar Zakat allocations.

4. Cryptographic Proof & Governance Ledger:
   - Every citation is bound to a SHA-256 source hash and Merkle root tree (perfected block height).
   - Multi-role governance protocol requires sequential cryptographic sign-offs from the Senior Credit Analyst, Shariah Compliance Unit (SCU) Officer, and Corporate Credit Committee.

5. Modern Frontend Experience:
   - Designed using the institutional UX Pilot design system with a persistent dark-navy command sidebar, responsive Internal Memorandum canvas, interactive liquidity stress visualizers, and portfolio intelligence telemetry feeds.
```

### 7. Shariah & Regulatory Compliance Statement
```
SANAD was explicitly designed to uphold Islamic banking principles under the guidance of the AAOIFI (Accounting and Auditing Organization for Islamic Financial Institutions) standards and Central Bank of Kuwait (CBK) corporate credit covenants:

- AAOIFI Standard No. 21 (Financial Papers & Investment):
  - Strictly screens corporate revenue to ensure prohibited income streams do not exceed the 5.0% threshold.
  - Automatically isolates conventional interest income and directs 100% of non-halal proceeds to Bait Al-Zakat Kuwait for purification (Taharah).
- Debt-to-Asset Capital Screening:
  - Continuously monitors the 30.0% conventional debt-to-asset ceiling and 33.0% liquid asset ratio.
- CBK Risk Regulations:
  - Enforces minimum 1.25x Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) floor and runs automated top-line stress shocks (-25% revenue downturn simulations).
- Sovereign Data Privacy:
  - Designed for CBK local compliance, eliminating cross-border data leakage through on-premise sanitization filters.
```

### 8. What We Learned & Challenges Overcome
```
1. Moving Beyond Surface-Level OCR:
   We learned early on that extracting data is not enough—banks fail when they approve loans with unrecorded encumbrances. We pivoted from a standard document summarizer to an adversarial multi-source circularization engine that actively seeks contradictions between filings.

2. Bridging Credit Underwriting with Shariah Purifications:
   Conventional banking tools treat non-compliant income as a simple error. In Islamic banking, it requires a legally binding Taharah calculation. Integrating AAOIFI Standard No. 21 directly into the automated financial model allowed us to make Shariah compliance a seamless part of the credit workflow rather than a bureaucratic blocker.

3. Achieving Institutional UI/UX:
   Bank executives and committee members do not want unstructured chat transcripts. Adopting the UX Pilot Internal Memorandum format provided the exact visual dignity and density required by Credit Committees and Shariah Boards.
```
