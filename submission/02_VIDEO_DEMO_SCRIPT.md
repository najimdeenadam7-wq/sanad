## Recording Tips for Maximum Impact
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 (Full HD) at 60fps.
- **Audio:** Use a clean USB microphone or headset; avoid room echo.
- **Mouse Movement:** Move the cursor deliberately. Hover over key numbers for 1-2 seconds so the judges can read them easily.
- **Pacing:** Speak at a steady, authoritative tempo. Do not rush.
